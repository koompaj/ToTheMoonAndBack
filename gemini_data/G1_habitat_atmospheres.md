### Notes
1. **Scope and Verification:** All 9 Task G1 prompt targets (a through i) were addressed with 16 distinct primary records. Every citation links directly to a verifiable record on `ntrs.nasa.gov` or `standards.nasa.gov`.
2. **Exploration Atmosphere Confirmation (Item e):** The 8.2 psia total pressure and 34% O2 concentration (balance 66% N2) was confirmed. It originated as an adjustment from an earlier 8.0 psia / 32% O2 recommendation (NASA/TP-2010-216134) to provide an 11 mmHg boost in alveolar oxygen partial pressure (PiO2 ~128 mmHg, equivalent to 4,000 ft altitude) while maintaining identical DCS risk. The standard control box tolerance is ±0.2 psia total pressure (8.0 to 8.4 psia) and ±2.0% O2 concentration (32.0% to 36.0%).
3. **Gateway and HLS (Item f):** Gateway requirement `L2-GW-0227` from *Gateway System Requirements* (`DSG-RQMT-001`, NTRS 20190029153) specifies an operating range of 65 kPa (9.5 psia) to 102 kPa (14.9 psia) for habitable spaces outside airlocks. HLS and lunar surface habitats baseline the 8.2 psia / 34% O2 exploration atmosphere to eliminate pre-breathe times before 4.3 psia EVA suit egress.
4. **Mars Architectures (Item g):** As documented in NASA/TP-2015-218570/REV2 (*Life Support Baseline Values and Assumptions Document*), Mars transit vehicles without planned EVAs maintain an Earth-like 101 kPa (14.7 psia) with 21% O2, whereas Mars surface habitats and pressurized rovers baseline 57 kPa (8.2 psia) with 34% O2 for high EVA cadence.
5. **Spacesuits (Item h):** Shuttle/ISS EMU operates at nominal 4.3 psia (4.3 psid) with 100% O2. The xEMU (Exploration EMU) and AxEMU (Axiom EMU) retain 4.3 psia/psid with 100% O2 as nominal working pressure, but incorporate variable electronic pressure regulation up to 8.2 psia/psid for pre-breathe reduction and in-suit DCS treatment.
6. **Flammability Documents (Item i):** NASA-STD-6001B governs material flammability certification under worst-case pressure and oxygen conditions (Test 1 upward flammability). NASA-STD-3001 Volume 2 governs crew habitable atmosphere limits and mandates inert diluent gases specifically to suppress flammability.
