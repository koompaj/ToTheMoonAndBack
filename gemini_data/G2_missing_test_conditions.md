### Notes
1. **Saffire-I vs Saffire-III Dimensions (Discrepancy Resolution)**: In NASA's summary table for Saffire-I (`PSI-98_Experimental table_SAFFIRE-1.csv`), the column headers for `Sample Length (cm)` and `Sample Width (cm)` are inverted (listing 40.6 cm length and 94 cm width). In contrast, the Saffire-III table (`PSI-100_Experimental table_SAFFIRE-3.csv`) and the definitive flight paper (D.L. Urban et al., *Combustion and Flame* 199 (2019) 168–182, DOI: 10.1016/j.combustflame.2018.10.012) correctly document the sample as **94 cm long by 40.6 cm wide** (the long axis aligned with the flow duct).
2. **Ambient Pressure in Microgravity Science Glovebox (MSG)**: BASS, BASS-II, SPICE, SLICE, SAME, and SAME-R all operated inside the MSG on board the ISS. The MSG working volume maintains a slight negative pressure relative to the cabin for particulate/fume containment, but the ambient static pressure is identical to the nominal ISS cabin pressure (~101.3 kPa / 1 atm / 14.7 psia).
3. **Oxygen Vitiation in BASS-II**: BASS-II was specifically designed to evaluate flammability boundaries at reduced oxygen by injecting nitrogen into the MSG working volume, systematically testing from ambient 22.2% down to 13.6% O2. For 3/8" clear PMMA rods, stable combustion was sustained down to 13.6% O2 in microgravity, which is lower than the limiting oxygen index in normal gravity downward burning (~15%–17%).
4. **Cygnus Vehicle Ambient Atmosphere**: Saffire-I, Saffire-II, and Saffire-III were conducted inside uncrewed Cygnus resupply vehicles following ISS departure prior to atmospheric re-entry. Initial ambient conditions were standard spacecraft cabin atmosphere: ~100 kPa total pressure and ~21% to 22% O2. Saffire-IV, V, and VI subsequently explored reduced-pressure, elevated-oxygen exploration atmospheres.
